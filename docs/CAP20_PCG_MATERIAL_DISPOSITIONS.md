# CAP-20 source dispositions: PCG and material slices

Status: bounded source review at Nexus `985426c`, with working-tree changes not used as evidence. These are dispositions of named **slices**, not whole-record classifications, route promotions, or live Unreal qualification. Source records from different providers and layers can overlap; do not add their counts or infer equivalence by name. The [capability ledger](../catalog/capability-coverage.json) remains the enumeration and executable routes remain in [operations](../catalog/operations.json).
