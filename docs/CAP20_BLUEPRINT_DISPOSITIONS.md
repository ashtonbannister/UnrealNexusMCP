## Selected compile and session boundary

The current catalog selects `blueprint.compile.memory.asset` and `blueprint.compile.memory.session` through Claireon with the reviewed `claireon-nexus-no-autosave-v1` compatibility patch. Claireon's native [compile operation](https://github.com/believer-oss/Claireon/blob/89bee2f4c62d1df625cca71ae66e7c81a415a009/Source/Claireon/Private/Tools/ClaireonBlueprintGraphTool_Compile.cpp#L125-L180) uses `SkipSave` and compiler diagnostics, but the enclosing public wrapper's autosave behavior must also be neutralized by the reviewed patch and live marker; source and fixture evidence are not proof of the loaded binary or disk behavior. The Claireon Blueprint internal compile declaration is **not** in the currently enumerated Claireon ledger subset; no ledger key is invented for it.

The [session comparison](CAP02_SOURCE_REVIEW.md) records distinct open, close, force-release and lease semantics. Its source findings do not establish an authenticated client-owner lock or a safe owner-scoped close. Session internals not enumerated in the bounded ledger remain unknown, not rejected as duplicates. [CAP-07/08](CAP07_08_SOURCE_REVIEW.md) records additional candidates and the fixture and native acceptance matrix; unreviewed APIs, the other selectors folded into each row, applicable component licenses, exact runtime surfaces and engine behavior remain open.
